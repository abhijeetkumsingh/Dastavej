How it works

Three steps, no account.

1

Add your file

Drag it in, browse for it, or capture it with your camera.

2

Choose your settings

Pick a size, page range, format or role. You'll see exactly what was done.

3

Download the result

Get the finished file straight away. It is removed from the server automatically.

Privacy

Your documents are not a library. They are temporary.

  • No login, no registration and no permanent document storage.
  • Each upload is saved under a random name, visible only to your browser session, and deleted automatically after {{ file_ttl }} minutes (or sooner when you delete it).
  • Scanning, PDF, image, conversion, OCR and resume tools run entirely on this server.
  • AI Document Assistant: only when you ask a question, summarise or analyse, the relevant text excerpts are sent to Google's Gemini API. You must confirm this each time before anything is sent.
  • The database keeps only technical job details (file sizes, page counts, expiry), never the contents of your documents.
